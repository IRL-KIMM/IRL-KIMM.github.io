Bio소개

Jang Ho Cho is a Principal Researcher and Head of the Department of Robot Applications at KIMM, and a Professor at the University of Science & Technology (UST). He received his B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST, and was a postdoctoral researcher at KAIST and at the Department of Automatic Control, Lund University, Sweden, before joining KIMM in 2013. His research centers on how robots physically interact with people, from the stability of time-delayed teleoperation and haptics to pHRI control, and on bringing that work into medical, rehabilitation, and diagnostic robots. He is now extending it toward Physical AI and lightweight AI for robot control. He has published 10 SCI(E) journal papers and 32 international conference papers, and holds 60 registered patents.

조장호 박사는 한국기계연구원 로봇응용연구실 연구실장(책임연구원)이자 UST 교수입니다. KAIST 기계공학과에서 학사·석사·박사 학위를 받았고, KAIST와 스웨덴 Lund University 자동제어학과에서 박사후연구원을 거쳐 2013년 한국기계연구원에 합류했습니다. 시간지연 원격조작의 안정성, 햅틱, 물리적 인간-로봇 상호작용 제어를 연구해 왔으며, 이를 의료·재활·진단 로봇에 적용하고 있습니다. 최근에는 로봇 제어를 위한 Physical AI와 경량 AI로 연구를 넓히고 있습니다. SCI(E) 논문 10편, 국제학술대회 논문 32편을 발표했고, 등록 특허 60건을 보유하고 있습니다.
